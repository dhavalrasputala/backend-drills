## Day 7: Load Balancer 
- My Explanation

- Load balance is mechanism in which incoming traffic is distributed over range of servers so that no one server has all the load and system doesn't malfunction due to that load.

## The Flow

- 100 Clients --> Load balancer --> distributed among 3 servers

## Trade-offs / Why it matters

- Improved performance due to distributed load among multiple individual units.
- Improved fault Tolerance , lowering the possibilty of downtime due to multiple units.
