## Day 6: Horizontal Scaling 
- My Explanation

- With increase in the number of users , load on server also increase to handle this load server has two option Horizontal Scaling & Vertical Scaling.

- Horizontal Scaling refers to process of increasing capabilites of system by increase the machinary or servers to distribute workload amongs large number of individual units. 

## The Flow

- 1 Server Carrying all the Trafic --> Load Distributed Amongs 3 Servers 

## Trade-offs / Why it matters

- Improved performance due to distributed load among multiple individual units.

- Improved fault Tolerance , lowering the possibilty of downtime due to multiple units.
